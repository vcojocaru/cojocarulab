Dear candidates, registration at the Integrative Biology Doctoral School takes place in two sessions: summer 10-16 July 2025 and autumn 8-11 september 2025. According to the methodology described on the website of the Institute of Doctoral Studies of UBB, registration is done online on the dedicated platform. PhD position on the application and development of physics-based and AI-based computational methods to decode and re-engineer interactions between biomolecules with the aim of generating novel knowledge that may contribute to future therapeutic applications.
